Types of funding

4 ways to fund your studies

Government scholarships

Fully funded awards from governments — tuition, stipend and often flights. Highly competitive.

University merit scholarships

Tuition discounts of 10–100% from universities, often given automatically with your offer.

Research funding

Assistantships, lab positions and PhD stipends that pay your tuition and a salary.

Need-based & external awards

Grants from foundations, trusts and companies based on need or background.

Myths that cost students scholarships

Don't fall for these

"I need Korean to apply for GKS"

No. TOPIK is not required to apply. GKS includes a Korean language year, and a TOPIK score can give you extra advantage.

"I'll apply after my final results"

Many scholarships (GKS, MEXT, Chevening) close months before final results. Plan 12–18 months ahead and apply with your current grades.

"Only toppers get scholarships"

Grades matter, but so do your SOP, motivation, research fit, leadership and recommendation letters.

"Agents can guarantee a scholarship"

No one can. Scholarships are decided by governments and universities. We help you put forward the strongest possible application.
