Questions

Frequently asked questions

When should I start planning to study abroad?

Ideally 12–18 months before your intake. Many scholarships (like GKS and MEXT) close months before results are out, so starting early gives you more options.

Do I need IELTS to study abroad?

Most English-taught programs ask for IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo. Some universities accept a medium-of-instruction letter instead. We'll tell you exactly what your chosen universities need.

Can you help me get a scholarship?

Yes — we help you find scholarships you're eligible for and prepare a strong application. Scholarships are competitive and decided by the university or government, so no counsellor can guarantee one.

Which countries do you work with?

We work with 650+ universities across 30+ countries, including the USA, UK, Ireland, Germany, France, Dubai, South Korea and more. If your dream university isn't mentioned on this site, just ask — we'll check for you.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Consultations are available online for students anywhere in the world, as well as face-to-face.

Do you also help with admissions in India?

Yes — we guide students for MBBS, engineering and management admissions in India, including clear, factual guidance on management quota.
